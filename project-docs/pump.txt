FOLLOW-UP SPLUNK SEARCHES — confirm why operator can't reach kraftcontroller:7777
====================================================================================

Round 1 verdict (from SEARCH-1/3/4/5): the Kafka reconciler in dah-flink-dev is stuck
on a precondition check — every ~45s the operator dials
  http://kraftcontroller-{0,1,2}.kraftcontroller.dah-flink-dev.svc.cluster.local:7777/jolokia/
to read LogEndOffset, every dial returns "dial tcp <pod-ip>:7777: i/o timeout", so it
returns early and never reaches the sts/kafka apply. Not a webhook, not a panic, not
a restart. The 2026-06-18T13:25:05Z "context deadline exceeded" on the CR status was
a one-shot API-server timeout that locked the condition; since then the reconciler
loops forever on the jolokia dial. SEARCH-5 confirms 46/50 events in last 2h are the
same "apply failed: waiting for at-least one kafka pod availability" at 15s cadence.

Three candidates for the :7777 timeout:
  1. Jolokia not listening inside the kraftcontroller pods (CFK 3.2.0 init container
     wiring issue) — does NOT require touching the confluent-operator namespace to fix.
  2. Egress NetworkPolicy in confluent-operator namespace blocking 7777 to dah-flink-dev
     — RBAC-blocked from the diagnostic SA, unknown.
  3. OpenShift OVN egress firewall at the SDN level.

Ingress on dah-flink-dev side is OK — allow-from-confluent-operator NetworkPolicy
permits 7777 (dump.txt L139). Run A first; A's outcome decides whether B/C are needed.
