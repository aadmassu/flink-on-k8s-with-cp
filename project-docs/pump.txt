BIG FINDING: authz at the CR level lives in this top-level
`spec.authorization` block in CFK 3.2 v1beta1 — NOT in
configOverrides.server. The production yaml was setting
`authorizer.class.name=ConfluentServerAuthorizer` and

explain kafka.spec.authorization.type` should give us the enum
list — add to diagnostics.yaml.
