Round 2 (Try B) — NEXT
----------------------
kafka-bisect.yaml now = minimal + configOverrides.server + spec.services.mds.
Why MDS first (out of the 3 remaining): it is the most exotic block in
the production spec — it makes CFK stand up an extra in-pod service on
:8090 and validates Okta endpoints at admission time. CFK 3.2.0 has more
surface area to choke on here than on listener blocks. Bonus: adding
services.mds fixes the Round-1 broker crash because CFK will then
auto-populate confluent.metadata.bootstrap.servers, so the RBAC
authorizer initializes cleanly. Required Secrets (both already present
in dah-flink-dev per Round 1 ESO-managed Secrets list):
  - mds-token-keypair  (Opaque, holds mdsPublicKey.pem + mdsTokenKeyPair.pem)
  - oauth-mds-rest     (Opaque, holds OAuth client creds for MDS)
